## 2. Product overview

AFF Marketplace is the commerce, vendor and sponsor platform behind African Food Festival Norway. Every sale at the festival runs through it, and every vendor gets a year-round business profile. The festival is how vendors and customers arrive; the platform is the long-term asset.

**Vision.** Become the digital infrastructure for African-owned businesses in Scandinavia, starting with AFFN.

**How it works at the event.** A visitor scans a vendor's QR code, sees the menu in the browser, pays with Vipps or card and collects the order when it is ready. The vendor sees the order on a kitchen screen. After the event, AFFN reconciles every vendor's sales, approves settlements, and the payment provider pays each vendor its sales minus commission.

**Business objectives**

| ID | Objective | Measure of success |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BO-01 | Digitise vendor onboarding | 100 % of vendors apply, upload documents and get approved in the platform |
| BO-02 | Run all festival sales through one checkout | Share of vendor sales paid through the platform |
| BO-03 | Collect AFFN commission automatically | Zero manual commission calculations at settlement |
| BO-04 | Prove sponsor value with data | Every sponsor receives a post-event report generated by the platform |
| BO-05 | Give vendors year-round visibility | Public vendor profiles stay live and collect leads after the event |
| BO-06 | Capture economic impact data | One-click economic impact report per festival edition |

Targets for BO-02 to BO-05 are open items (section 12).

## 4. Users, roles and tenancy

Five roles sign in; festival visitors never do. Roles are granted per organization, so the same model serves AFFN today and other event organizers under Lumina Connect later.

| Role | Scope | Can do | Cannot do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Platform Owner | Whole platform | Create organizations, see platform-wide health and billing | Edit a tenant's vendors or money without an audit-logged reason |
| Organization Admin (Super Admin) | One organization | Manage events, admins, commission defaults, approve settlements | — |
| Event Admin (AFFN Admin) | One organization, assigned events | Review vendors, assign stalls, run operations, prepare settlements | Approve settlements (four-eyes rule: preparer ≠ approver) |
| Vendor | Own business, events it is approved for | Profile, products, orders, sales, statements, leads | See other vendors' data |
| Sponsor | Own sponsorship | Package, assets, sponsor QR scans, reports | See vendor sales |
| Visitor (no account) | Public pages | Browse, order, pay, track an order by link, send a lead | — |

**Tenancy model.** Organization → Event (festival edition, e.g. AFFN 2027) → everything else. A vendor business is a platform-level entity that can take part in many events; its participation, stall, products-for-sale, orders and money are scoped to one event. Every table carries `organization_id`, and every event-scoped table carries `event_id`, enforced by row-level security.

## 5. Payment and settlement architecture

Customer money goes straight to a licensed payment provider (PSP), which holds it on the vendor's behalf. AFFN's commission is withheld automatically at payment time, and vendor payouts stay paused until AFFN approves the settlement after the event. This is the only model that keeps AFFN outside the payment-licence requirement Finanstilsynet enforced against Ski-VM Trondheim 2025.

Money flow: Visitor pays NOK 500 → licensed PSP splits it → vendor balance NOK 437.50 (payouts paused until AFFN approval) and AFFN platform fee NOK 62.50 (50.00 + 12.50 mva) → each paid out to its own bank account.

The PSP splits each payment on the spot; only the vendor's share waits for AFFN's settlement approval.

**Payment flow**

1. The visitor scans the vendor's QR code and opens the vendor page in the browser.
2. Checkout creates an order (`pending_payment`) and a PSP payment that names the vendor's PSP account and a platform fee equal to commission plus mva.
3. The visitor pays with Vipps, card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. The PSP confirms by webhook; the order moves to `paid` and appears on the vendor's kitchen screen.
4. The vendor's share sits in the vendor's PSP balance with payouts paused. AFFN's fee lands in AFFN's PSP balance.
5. After the event closes, the platform reconciles its ledger against the PSP's reports and lists any exceptions (refunds, disputes, mismatches).
6. An Event Admin prepares a settlement batch. An Organization Admin approves it (two different people).
7. The platform tells the PSP to release each vendor's payout, then issues the vendor statement and AFFN's commission invoice.

**Provider-agnostic adapter.** All PSP calls go through one interface, so Stripe Connect or Swedbank Pay can be plugged in once quotes are in:

| Operation | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `onboardVendor` | Create the vendor's PSP account and return the hosted KYC link |
| `getVendorStatus` | Read KYC and payout eligibility |
| `createPayment` | Charge the customer with a platform fee, idempotent per order |
| `handleWebhook` | Verify signature, update payment and order state |
| `refund` | Full or partial refund, reversing the platform fee pro rata |
| `releasePayout` | Pay out an approved settlement amount to the vendor |
| `fetchSettlementReport` | Pull PSP transactions for reconciliation |

**Internal ledger.** The platform keeps its own double-entry ledger as the source of truth for reporting. Each paid order writes entries for gross amount, commission, mva on commission and PSP fee. The commission rate is snapshotted on the order, so a later rate change never alters past sales.

**Refunds and disputes.** A refund before settlement reduces the vendor's pending balance and reverses AFFN's fee pro rata. A dispute after payout is deducted from the vendor's next settlement or invoiced, per the vendor agreement.

**Fallback.** Vipps on Stripe Connect is in private preview. If Vipps access is not confirmed eight weeks before the festival, launch with card, Apple Pay and Google Pay, or choose Swedbank Pay.

## 6. Commission, fees and mva

A vendor on "10 % + mva" keeps NOK 437.50 of a NOK 500 sale. AFFN pays the payment fee out of its own NOK 50 commission.

**Worked example: NOK 500 sale, vendor not mva-registered, commission 10 % + mva**

| Line | Amount (NOK) | Who gets it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gross sale paid by customer | 500.00 | — |
| Commission (10 %) | 50.00 | AFFN |
| Mva on commission (25 %) | 12.50 | AFFN, then paid to Skatteetaten |
| Platform fee withheld by PSP | 62.50 | AFFN's PSP balance |
| **Vendor payout** | **437.50** | Vendor (effective rate 12.5 %) |
| PSP fee, illustrative 2.5 % | 12.50 | Paid by AFFN out of its commission |
| AFFN net commission | 37.50 | AFFN |

The PSP fee rate is illustrative until quotes from Stripe and Swedbank Pay are in.

**Commission rules**

- Types: percentage, fixed amount per order, or hybrid (percentage plus fixed).
- Rate precedence: vendor-specific → product category → event default → organization default.
- Every rate has an effective-from date. The rate in force is snapshotted onto each order at payment time.
- Commission and its mva are calculated per order in øre, rounded half-up. Statement totals are the sum of order lines, never recalculated on the total.
- Rate changes require an Organization Admin and are written to the audit log with the reason.

**Mva rules**

- Commission always carries 25 % mva, shown as a separate line on every vendor statement and invoice.
- Products carry an `mva_rate` field, default 0 % because vendors are assumed not mva-registered. A registered vendor sets its own rate, and receipts then show mva.
- The vendor dashboard warns at NOK 40,000 and NOK 50,000 of platform sales in a rolling 12 months, the point at which the vendor must register for mva. Registering remains the vendor's responsibility.

**AFFN documents**

- **Commission invoice**: one per vendor per settlement, with commission, mva and "settled by deduction from payout". Sequential numbering and all fields required by bokføringsforskriften.
- **Stall fee invoice**: issued on vendor approval and paid by payment link into AFFN's own account (this is AFFN's own revenue). Stall assignment is confirmed only after payment. Mva treatment is an open item (section 12).
- **Vendor statement**: gross sales, refunds, commission, mva on commission, adjustments, net payout, with order-level detail as CSV.

## 8. Data architecture

The schema is tenant-ready from the first migration: every row belongs to an organization, and every festival-specific row also belongs to an event. Money tables are append-only. Postgres on Supabase, with row-level security on every table.

| Domain | Core tables | Key rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tenancy | `organizations`, `events`, `memberships`, `platform_admins` | Event = festival edition (AFFN 2027). Membership carries the role per organization. |
| Vendors | `vendors`, `vendor_documents`, `event_vendors`, `vendor_agreements` | `vendors` is the business (org number unique). `event_vendors` is its participation in one event: status, stall, commission override, PSP account. |
| Review | `application_reviews`, `internal_notes` | One row per status change with actor and note. |
| Stalls | `stalls`, `stall_assignments` | One active assignment per stall per event. |
| Catalogue | `products`, `product_categories` | Products belong to the vendor; availability is per event. |
| Commerce | `orders`, `order_items`, `payments`, `refunds` | Item price, name and mva copied onto the order line at purchase. |
| Money | `commission_rules`, `ledger_entries`, `settlements`, `settlement_lines`, `payouts`, `invoices` | Ledger is double-entry and append-only; corrections are new entries. Amounts stored as integer øre. |
| Fees | `stall_fees`, `fee_payments` | AFFN's own revenue, kept apart from vendor money. |
| Sponsors | `sponsors`, `sponsor_packages`, `sponsorships`, `sponsor_assets`, `tracked_links` | Packages configurable per event. |
| Engagement | `qr_scans`, `page_views`, `leads` | Scans and views stored without IP addresses or personal data. |
| Platform | `audit_log`, `notifications`, `psp_webhook_events` | Webhook events stored raw and processed idempotently. |

**Rules that apply everywhere**

- Primary keys are UUIDs; timestamps are `timestamptz` in UTC, shown in Europe/Oslo.
- RLS policies read the caller's memberships; the service role is used only in server-side functions.
- Personal data lives in clearly marked columns so it can be exported or erased per GDPR without touching financial records, which are kept for 5 years under bokføringsloven.

## 9. Norway compliance

The platform is designed so AFFN needs no financial licence, and so every vendor's bookkeeping is complete without extra work. Items marked "confirm" need a written check from an accountant or lawyer before launch.

| Area | Rule | What the platform does | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Payment services | Finansforetaksloven: only licensed firms may receive money to pass on to sellers (Finanstilsynet on Ski-VM 2025) | Licensed PSP holds all vendor funds; AFFN only receives its platform fee | Designed in |
